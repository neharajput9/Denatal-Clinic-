Expert Dental Care

Our Dental Services

Comprehensive treatments for every dental need — modern, gentle, and effective.

Teeth Cleaning & Scaling

Professional cleaning removes plaque, tartar, and surface stains to restore your teeth's natural shine and protect gum health.

  • Ultrasonic scaling & root planing
  • Polishing & stain removal
  • Gum disease prevention

Teeth Whitening

Brighten your smile by several shades with our in-office laser and take-home whitening kits using safe, proven techniques.

  • In-chair laser whitening
  • Custom take-home trays
  • Sensitivity-free treatment

Root Canal Treatment

Save your natural tooth with painless root canal therapy using modern rotary endodontic techniques and digital imaging.

  • Single & multi-visit options
  • Rotary endodontic system
  • Post-treatment crown placement

Braces & Clear Aligners

Achieve a perfectly straight smile with traditional metal braces or virtually invisible clear aligners tailored for your case.

  • Metal & ceramic braces
  • Clear aligner therapy
  • Retainer & follow-up care

Dental Implants

Replace missing teeth permanently with titanium implants that look, feel, and function just like your natural teeth.

  • Single tooth implants
  • Implant-supported bridges
  • All-on-4 / All-on-6

Paediatric Dentistry

Friendly and gentle dental care specially designed for children, ensuring a positive experience and healthy dental habits from a young age.

  • Milk tooth extraction
  • Fluoride treatments
  • Pit & fissure sealants

Dental Crowns & Bridges

Restore the shape, strength, and appearance of damaged or missing teeth with high-quality ceramic, zirconia, or PFM crowns and bridges.

  • Zirconia & ceramic crowns
  • Porcelain fused to metal
  • Fixed dental bridges

Dentures & Removable Prosthetics

Custom-fitted complete and partial dentures to restore your smile's function and aesthetics comfortably and affordably.

  • Full & partial dentures
  • Flexible valplast dentures
  • Denture repairs & relines

Oral Surgery

Safe and professional surgical procedures including wisdom tooth removal, cyst removal, and complex extractions with minimal discomfort.

  • Wisdom tooth removal
  • Surgical extractions
  • Minor oral surgeries
